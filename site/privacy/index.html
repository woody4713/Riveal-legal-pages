Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Riveal is provided by woody4713. This policy explains the information Riveal handles when you use the app, including its optional AI features. For privacy questions or requests, email woody4713@gmail.com.

Information Riveal handles

  • Account information. You may start as a guest or sign in with Apple or Google. Riveal handles the identifiers, authentication information, and any profile details these sign-in services provide to establish and maintain your account.
  • Information you enter. This includes tasks, plans, scheduling preferences, onboarding answers, execution history, assistant settings, and conversations you choose to create. Riveal stores information on your device and, when you use a connected account or guest session, synchronizes relevant information with its cloud services.
  • Technical and usage records. Riveal handles device and session identifiers, synchronization and request identifiers, error and security records, and AI usage records such as the model used, token counts, status, and calculated cost. Visits to these legal pages may generate web request logs, such as IP address and request metadata, at the hosting provider. These records support the service, prevent duplicate operations, investigate failures, and apply free AI usage limits.
  • AI requests. When you choose an AI feature, relevant task, planning, or conversation content is sent from Riveal's service to DeepSeek to produce a response or suggestion. AI features may include task parsing, conversation, planning assistance, and estimate suggestions. Review AI output before applying it. You can use manual task and planning paths without submitting an AI request.

How Riveal uses information

Riveal uses this information to provide sign-in, save and synchronize your work, generate and display plans, respond to AI requests you initiate, apply free AI usage limits, secure accounts, and diagnose service problems.

Service providers and locations

Riveal uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data, Render for application services, and DeepSeek for AI requests you initiate. Apple or Google handles the sign-in option you choose. These providers receive information needed for their roles and may process it under their own service arrangements. Riveal's current primary cloud and application services are configured in Singapore. Depending on the provider and feature, information may also be processed in other countries or regions.

Storage and deletion

Riveal keeps account and product information while it is needed to provide your account and features. Signing out ends the active session on that device; it does not delete cloud account data. The in-app Delete account and all data flow is designed to delete the account and associated active product data and revoke its sessions. A limited set of technical deletion and recovery receipts may remain for up to 30 days. Copies in service-provider backups or operational logs may remain temporarily until their normal retention cycles end.

To request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask about retention, email woody4713@gmail.com. Please identify the Riveal account or guest installation involved, but do not send a password. Riveal may need to verify your connection to the account before acting. Applicable local law may provide additional rights.

Security

Riveal uses HTTPS for online connections and account-scoped access controls for cloud information. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Age and policy changes

Riveal is intended for people aged 14 or older. If you believe a child under 14 has used Riveal, contact woody4713@gmail.com so the account and related data can be reviewed.

Riveal may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. For material changes, Riveal will provide notice through the app or another appropriate channel when required by applicable law.
